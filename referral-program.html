How tracking and payout eligibility work

The mechanics behind the QR code, so partners know what's actually being measured before they hand out a brochure.

Step 1

Referral code assigned

Each approved partner gets a unique referral code tied to their business record, used on their personalized brochure and QR route.

Step 2

Touchpoints get attributed

Website visits, form starts, checkout starts, and completed payments through that code are logged against the partner's record.

Step 3

Admin review before payout

A completed, paid project is reviewed manually before any invoice or transfer is issued - referrals aren't auto-paid on click volume alone.

Payout terms, commission structure, and qualifying project types are confirmed directly with each partner during approval rather than published as a blanket rate - project scope varies too much (a website build and an automation project are priced differently) for one generic number to be accurate.

Referral Program FAQ

Common questions before applying.

Eligibility

Who gets approved?

Businesses that regularly and legitimately meet new business owners - sign shops, print shops, bookkeepers, consultants, and similar local vendors are the strongest fit.

Tracking

What if a referral calls instead of scanning the QR code?

Mention the referring partner during intake and it can be manually attributed. The QR code and referral link make tracking automatic, but they aren't the only path to credit.

Timing

How long does partner approval take?

Applications are reviewed manually, not auto-approved, since partners appear in live referral materials. There's no published guaranteed turnaround - reach out if a submitted application is time-sensitive.

Fit

Who is this program not a good fit for?

Anyone expecting a flat monthly payout unrelated to actual referrals, or anyone unwilling to be reviewed before appearing in referral materials. This is a performance-based partner program, not a paid placement.

Materials

Do I have to use the printed brochure?

No. The brochure is one option. A partner's unique referral link or QR code works the same way whether it's on a printed handout, a counter display, or shared digitally.

Status

Can I check on a referral I already sent?

Yes, through the partner verification dashboard linked above, once your application has been approved and your code is active.

Payout

How and when do partners actually get paid?

Today that's a manual invoice or transfer after a referred project is completed and paid, reviewed case by case. We're not going to publish a fake automated-payout promise before that layer is actually built.

Scale

Is this open to partners outside Tampa Bay?

The program started with local sign shops, print shops, and service vendors who meet new business owners in person, but the same tracked-code approach works for any partner who regularly refers businesses that need a website, SEO, or automation.
