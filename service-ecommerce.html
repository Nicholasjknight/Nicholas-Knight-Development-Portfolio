E-Commerce FAQs

Common questions from businesses before starting a store build or migration.

How much does an e-commerce website cost in Tampa Bay?

Store Preview is $750, Payment-Link Store is $1,200, E-Commerce Launch is $2,997, and Growth Store is $3,997 — all flat projects for their listed scope. Advanced E-Commerce System starts from $7,500 and is scoped for complex subscriptions, fulfillment, marketplace, migration, or integration work.

Do you build on Shopify or custom code?

Both, depending on what fits. Shopify suits larger catalogs needing their ecosystem. For lean, fast storefronts without monthly platform fees, a custom-coded static site with Snipcart or Stripe Checkout often performs better and costs less to run.

Can you migrate an existing store?

Yes. Product data, customer records, URLs, and SEO equity can all be migrated with proper redirects and data mapping. The migration plan is locked in before a single line is written so nothing gets lost.

What payment processors do you integrate with?

Stripe and Snipcart for custom-code builds. Shopify Payments, PayPal, and Stripe for Shopify projects. Subscriptions, split payments, or in-person POS sync are scoped individually based on requirements.

Can you automate inventory, pricing, and product updates?

Yes. Product imports, pricing sync, stock monitoring, feed cleanup, and reporting pipelines can all be automated so store operations do not depend on manual spreadsheet updates.

How long does a custom e-commerce build take?

A focused store with a defined catalog and checkout flow can often launch in a few weeks. Larger catalogs, migrations, account systems, and automation-heavy stores take longer. Catalog complexity and integration count drive the timeline most.

Do you work with businesses outside Tampa Bay?

Yes. Tampa Bay is the primary local target area, but Knight Logics can build or migrate stores for businesses anywhere. The local emphasis helps search discovery; it does not limit who the service is for.
