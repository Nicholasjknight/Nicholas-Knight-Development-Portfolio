How to use this library

Each case study is written like a commercial page — not a portfolio tile. Open the full study when you need scores, URLs, workflow detail, or labeled demo boundaries.

Match proof to your bottleneck

If leads are weak, start with live client builds (Roof Monsters, Screen Team, Faith Works, Breaking Ground) and website services. If the site works but the inbox is chaos, read CRM Outreach, Vendoroo dispatch, and ticketing workflows. If referrals or social are the gap, jump to Referral Network or Social Poster studies before buying another page.

Live client vs internal system vs demo

Live client cards link to published domains with measurable technical scores. Systems & ops document production engines we run daily — OutreachEngine, KG Dispatch, Knight Command, referral payouts. Portfolio & demos are labeled honestly (Farrell Electric preview, hospitality pattern) so sales samples never masquerade as ranking proof.

Industry filters (quick map)

  • Handyman / multi-trade: Knight Group, Vendoroo dispatch
  • Land clearing / heavy outdoor: Faith Works, Breaking Ground (demolition-first)

What you will not find here

We do not publish fabricated client revenue charts or stock photography passed off as local proof. Individual studies use live captures, walkthrough video, or a clearly labeled mock of a real queue — never stock as local proof. For package-level scope, see Authority Site and sibling package pages.
