Lane 2 — API + Playwright publish

Common questions

Which platforms use API vs Playwright?

X and Facebook use API clients where stable; LinkedIn and Nextdoor typically use Playwright runners; GBP posts use the Business Profile API for local cadence.

What outcomes matter beyond the scheduling UI?

Published posts landing on schedule, last-success timestamps updating per platform, GBP posts aligned to site campaigns, and failure counters visible during weekly operator review — not queue depth alone.

How do you keep brands isolated?

Separate queues and credential vaults per company (KL/KG/ST/faithworks) so one brand’s content cannot post to another account by accident.

Do you create content?

Systems focus — queue and posting infrastructure. Content can be supplied by your team, field job composites, or scoped separately.

Where do failures show?

Social Poster UI, operator queue health view, and Knight Command Logs tab — not silent email-only alerts after a week of quiet accounts.

Who is this for?

Tampa Bay multi-brand operators who need social scheduling with LinkedIn/Nextdoor coverage, GBP posts, and visible failure reporting beside CRM and email lanes.
