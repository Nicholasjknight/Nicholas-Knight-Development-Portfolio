Demo Preview FAQs

Will Google find this site?

No. Demo Preview is intentionally noindex and is not submitted to Search Console. That is the point — validation without pretending you launched.

Does the $200 expire?

It applies as credit when you upgrade to a larger Knight Logics website or Growth package. Ask at checkout if you have timing questions.

Can I put my own domain on it later?

Domain, DNS, SSL, and search foundation live on Local Site and above — or as part of Growth Website+Systems. The demo itself stays on the preview URL.

Is this the same as Preview Launch?

No. Preview Launch ($750) is a deeper multi-page proof (up to ~20 pages). Demo is the cheapest validation step.

What assets do you need from me?

Phone photos or Facebook albums, service list, service area, phone number, and any logo if you have one. We can start from imperfect assets — that is normal for Demo Preview.

How long does it take?

Demo Preview is designed to move quickly after intake and payment. Exact timing depends on how fast assets arrive and how clear the offer notes are.

What you should use the demo for

Show it to a spouse, partner, or early customer and ask: Is the offer clear? Would you call this number? Does this look like our business? If the answer is no, we adjust before you spend on a domain launch. If the answer is yes, upgrade with credit applied so the $200 was not a dead end.

Do not treat the GitHub Pages URL as your permanent marketing site. It is a scaffold. Local Site and above put the brand on your domain with search foundation. Growth adds the ops layer when leads need tracking and follow-up — including Systems-only pricing when the website is already good enough.
