Which business segment actually needs Authority Network

Most businesses never need 60–100+ pages. This tier is for real multi-market or multi-service coverage — not for inflating a page count to look bigger.

Multi-location trades and franchises

Roofing, electrical, screen enclosure, excavation, or handyman operators covering many cities across a metro need a page for each real service × location combination — not one page trying to rank everywhere. See roofing, electrician, screen enclosure, and excavation growth-system patterns for the vertical version of this framework.

Multi-brand contractor groups

Holding companies or multi-crew operators running several trade lines under one umbrella need a hub-and-spoke architecture where each service line gets its own conversion path without duplicating the same page five times. Contractor Growth Systems covers the parent-network pattern.

Regional service networks with proof depth

Businesses with genuine project photography, reviews, and case history across many markets can support a 60–100+ page network because each expansion page has real proof to show — not just a swapped city name. Thin proof is the most common reason this tier gets scoped down to Max Authority instead.

Restaurant, hospitality, and multi-unit operators

Groups running multiple locations or a hospitality brand with several service lines (catering, events, delivery) can use the same core-plus-expansion pattern. See restaurant & bar growth systems for the hospitality-specific version.

Authority Network FAQs

Why is this SCOPED instead of flat checkout?

60–100+ page networks vary wildly by market count, service depth, and content readiness. Scope protects both sides from surprise page counts.

Can I pay a deposit first?

Yes. Start with a $2,500 scope deposit via checkout, or book a consult if you need architecture guidance first.

Does this include monthly CRM?

No. This is a large website project. Ongoing ops is Growth from $397/mo.

What if I only need ~40 pages?

Max Authority is the flat $4,500 PROJECT lane for up to 40 pages.

Will expansion pages be thin doorway clones?

No. The governed framework varies real content per market and service — proof, local specifics, and CTA framing change page to page. A page that only swaps a city name gets flagged in milestone QA, not shipped.

How is the final page count decided?

The research + architecture call maps real service × market combinations against your proof inventory. We do not build to an arbitrary round number; we build to the page graph that earns its keep.

Can I start smaller and grow into Network later?

Yes. Many Network builds start as Max Authority or Local Site and expand once the core conversion system proves itself and coverage genuinely needs to scale.
