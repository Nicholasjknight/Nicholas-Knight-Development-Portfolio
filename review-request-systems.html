Lane 4 — Throttle & log

Log who was asked — skip duplicates — stay policy-safe

Reputation management for small business is timing and logging — not buying reviews or blasting every contact in a CRM export. Throttle rules prevent duplicate asks; opt-out handling respects compliance; monthly velocity vs job volume tells owners whether reputation is keeping pace with completed work.

  1. Request logging Log sent requests and completed reviews where visibility allows — no spreadsheet guesswork.
  2. Throttle rules Repeat customers and recent askers are suppressed — one ethical ask per job cycle.
  3. Failure visibility When a send fails, operators see it — not a silent queue drop during vacation weeks.

Common questions

When should review requests send?

Usually at job completion or shortly after payment — when customers are happiest — with throttle rules so nobody gets duplicate asks. Staff-triggered buttons work when full automation is phased in later.

Do you buy reviews?

Never — only ethical request timing, correct GBP links, and logged asks tied to finished work. Buying reviews violates platform policy and destroys long-term map trust.

SMS or email requests?

Either scoped per client; messages stay short and infrequent. One tap to the correct GBP review URL — not a weekly blast to your entire contact export.

What is the embedded map and reviews carousel?

A hand-coded on-site block showing your Google map pin and live review proof — so website visitors see trust signals without leaving for Google first. Pairs with GBP growth from post-job asks.

Integration with job tracker?

Ideal trigger is job completion status from ticketing or dispatch workflow when available. See job workflows and workflow automation for closeout hooks.

How does this relate to GBP services and local visibility?

Review requests are one lane in the local visibility stack. GBP services covers profile setup, categories, and posts; local visibility systems covers schema, geography pages, and map-pack SEO. Reviews compound both.

Who is this for?

Tampa Bay home-service and contractor teams with satisfied customers but flat Google review velocity — especially trades already investing in local visibility and GBP alignment.
