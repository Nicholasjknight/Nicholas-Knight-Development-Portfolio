The problem: outreach lists are either fake-cheap or expensive

Agencies and freelancers doing cold outreach usually end up choosing between two bad options. Option one is a stale, purchased list scraped from who-knows-where, full of duplicates, dead businesses, and contacts who never opted into anything. Option two is paying a research VA or a data vendor by the hour to manually build a fresh list for one niche and one city — slow, and the price scales badly once you need a second market.

The Local Opportunity Intelligence Pack is a third option: a fixed-price, automated research run against public business listing data for a niche and location you choose, scored by basic website-gap signals so the businesses most worth calling float to the top of the CSV. It is not the same product as our Playwright/axe/Lighthouse site audits — this pack looks outward at fifty prospects, not inward at your own site.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as a website audit?

No. This pack researches other businesses in your chosen niche and area. If you want an audit of your own site, use the $39 Health Audit instead.

Are the emails and phone numbers verified?

No. Fields are pulled from public listings as published. We do not run email verification or guarantee deliverability — treat contact fields as a starting point for your own outreach process.

Can I get more than 50 businesses?

The published SKU is 50 distinct businesses per order. For larger or recurring research needs, book a consult to scope a custom engagement.

What if my niche + city combination is too small?

Some rural or highly specific combinations may not have 50 distinct public businesses to find. If that happens, you get the largest honest, deduped list we can assemble — or a refund if the pack fails the quality gate entirely.

Do you send the outreach for me?

No. This is a research deliverable — CSV and HTML in your inbox. If you want us to run the outreach campaign itself, see Growth Systems Offer.

Can agencies resell this list to clients?

Yes, the CSV and HTML are yours to use internally or pass to a client as part of your own deliverable. For agency-branded audit PDFs specifically, see the White-Label Audit.
