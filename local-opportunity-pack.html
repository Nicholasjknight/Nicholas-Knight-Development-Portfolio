What this Instant Pack actually does

Maps shows businesses. This pack exports a ranked outreach list: niche + location → public listings → website contact enrichment → email MX / DNS checks → US phone format validation → credibility ranking → CSV + HTML in your inbox.

Email: mx_ok means the domain accepts mail (not a guarantee the inbox is monitored). Phone: valid_format means NANP format — not carrier lookup. Ranking: OpenAI (gpt-4o-mini by default) on every paid pack — included in the $49 price.

Still not included: cold-email sending, reply handling, or a human operator opening the CRM daily. For that operated lane, see CRM outreach / Growth Systems.

Frequently asked questions

Why not just use Google Maps?

Maps is free for browsing. This pack exports a ranked list with enrichment and contact checks so you are not manually opening fifty tabs. If that automation is not worth $49, skip it.

Do you verify emails and phones?

Emails are MX/DNS checked (mx_ok). Phones are validated for US NANP format (valid_format). That is not the same as carrier lookup or guaranteed inbox monitoring. Quality gates require enough contactable rows or the order refunds.

Is an LLM used?

Yes on every paid Instant Opportunity Pack. The fulfillment worker loads the operator OpenAI key and ranks prospects with gpt-4o-mini (overridable via OPENAI_MODEL). That costs pennies per pack and is included in the $49 price.

Is this the same as a website audit?

No. This pack researches other businesses in your niche/area. For your own URL, use the $39 Health Audit.

Can I get more than 50 businesses?

The published SKU is 50 distinct businesses per order. For larger or recurring research needs, book a consult to scope a custom engagement.

What if my niche + city combination is too small?

Some rural or highly specific combinations may not have 50 distinct public businesses to find. If that happens, you get the largest honest, deduped list we can assemble — or a refund if the pack fails the quality gate entirely.

Do you send the outreach for me?

No. This is a research deliverable — CSV and HTML in your inbox. If you want us to run the outreach campaign itself, see Growth Systems Offer.

Can agencies resell this list to clients?

Yes, the CSV and HTML are yours to use internally or pass to a client as part of your own deliverable. For agency-branded audit PDFs specifically, see the White-Label Audit.
