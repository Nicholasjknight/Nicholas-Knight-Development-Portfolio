Why Growth pricing is split

The outcome is a working lead-and-operations layer, not merely more pages. A named Local- or Authority-equivalent site scope is real project work and operations systems are different work, so clients with a usable site should not be charged for rebuilding it.

Five different commercial lanes

Use the lane that matches the bottleneck. These are not interchangeable names for the same deliverable.

Website + Systems

A project setup plus monthly management when both public conversion architecture and back-office follow-up need rebuilding.

  • Includes the scoped website build
  • Adds tracking, sequences, dashboards, and tier-specific operations
  • Best upgrade from a site-only Authority or Max project when lead handling becomes the bottleneck

Systems-only

A lower setup price plus monthly management when the existing site can stay and only forms, CRM, follow-up, reporting, or field workflows need integration.

  • No duplicate website rebuild
  • Current forms and data destinations are audited before connection
  • Large corrective site work is quoted separately

One-time Automation Build

A scoped implementation with a defined handoff when you need one workflow built but do not want ongoing Growth management.

  • Examples: intake routing, a report pipeline, or a bounded internal tool
  • Includes agreed implementation and handoff documentation
  • Monitoring, content cadence, campaign operation, and future changes are excluded unless separately purchased

Monthly Visibility

Ongoing site, content, or Google Business Profile care for a business whose lead operations already work.

  • Visibility and maintenance, not a CRM build
  • No field app, custom automation, or website rebuild by default
  • Upgrade to Systems-only when follow-up and attribution become the constraint

Field Ops

A custom mobile workflow for crews that need jobs, photos, estimates, signatures, invoices, dispatch, or payment status on the phone.

  • Individually scoped around real field steps and roles
  • Can be Website+Systems or field systems-only
  • Hardware, carrier plans, and third-party seats are excluded

How a Growth engagement runs

Whether you buy Website+Systems or Systems-only, the sequence is deliberate: understand the bottleneck, ship the minimum system that closes it, then run monthly care so it does not rot.

  1. Intake & scopeWe confirm whether your site stays or gets rebuilt, which tier fits, and which automation lane matters first.
  2. FoundationForms, tracking, inboxes, and data destinations get wired so leads are not landing in a black hole.
  3. BuildSite (if purchased), tracker, sequences, dashboards, and any field app modules for your tier.
  4. Launch & handoffYou get working flows, not a slide deck. Operators know where to look each morning.
  5. Monthly careRetainer covers upkeep, reporting, and iteration — cancel anytime; the point is continuity, not lock-in theater.

Who this is for — and who should start smaller

Good fit

  • Trades and local services with enough volume that missed follow-up costs real jobs
  • Owners who already tried “just a website” and still cannot see lead sources or close the loop
  • Teams ready for a monthly ops relationship, not a one-and-done brochure
  • Companies that need a field phone app more than another WordPress theme

Start elsewhere first

  • Still validating the offer? Demo Preview ($200) or Preview Launch ($750)
  • Need a solid domain-backed site without ops? Local Site, Authority, or Max Authority
  • Only need map posts and light edits? Monthly Visibility or GBP Maintenance

Price drivers

  • Whether a website rebuild is included or the current site can stay
  • Number of forms, sources, inboxes, roles, brands, and data destinations
  • Sequence, outreach, reporting, social, and field-app modules in scope
  • Legacy data migration, third-party integrations, permissions, measurement, and QA depth

Growth System FAQs

Do I have to buy a new website?

No. Choose Systems-only when the current site is usable. Choose Website+Systems when page depth, structure, or conversion paths are part of the problem.

Why is monthly required?

Growth systems rot without care — sequences break, forms change, dashboards go stale. The monthly fee is active management for that tier, not hosting theater. Cancel anytime.

Can I upgrade from Starter to Full later?

Yes. Many clients start Starter (or Systems-only Starter), then add an automation lane or Field Ops when the bottleneck is clear.

Where do OutreachEngine, Email-Agent, and Social live?

They are part of Full / Field Ops scope — not sold as vague $497 middle SKUs on the pricing page. See Growth Systems overview, CRM Outreach, and Social systems.
