Why Growth pricing is split

A 70+ page site build is real project work. Ops systems are different work. Charging the same when you already have a site would overcharge you for pages you do not need.

Website + Systems

Best when the public site is thin, outdated, or missing service/area depth. You get a deep custom-coded site plus the ops stack for that tier. Setup starts at $5,000 (Starter/Full) or $10,000 (Field Ops), plus monthly care.

  • New or rebuilt marketing site in scope
  • Search foundation / GBP depth per tier
  • Same monthly retainer as Systems-only for that tier

Systems-only

Best when the website is already usable and the bottleneck is follow-up, tracking, reviews, outreach, or field ops. Setup drops because we are not rebuilding the marketing site: from $2,500 (Starter), from $3,500 (Full), from $8,000 (Field Ops), plus the same monthly rates.

  • Integrates with your current site and forms
  • Still includes monthly support for that tier
  • We may recommend light site fixes if intake is broken — scoped separately if large

How a Growth engagement runs

Whether you buy Website+Systems or Systems-only, the sequence is deliberate: understand the bottleneck, ship the minimum system that closes it, then run monthly care so it does not rot.

  1. Intake & scopeWe confirm whether your site stays or gets rebuilt, which tier fits, and which automation lane matters first.
  2. FoundationForms, tracking, inboxes, and data destinations get wired so leads are not landing in a black hole.
  3. BuildSite (if purchased), tracker, sequences, dashboards, and any field app modules for your tier.
  4. Launch & handoffYou get working flows, not a slide deck. Operators know where to look each morning.
  5. Monthly careRetainer covers upkeep, reporting, and iteration — cancel anytime; the point is continuity, not lock-in theater.

Growth System FAQs

Do I have to buy a new website?

No. Choose Systems-only when the current site is usable. Choose Website+Systems when page depth, structure, or conversion paths are part of the problem.

Why is monthly required?

Growth systems rot without care — sequences break, forms change, dashboards go stale. The monthly fee is active management for that tier, not hosting theater. Cancel anytime.

Can I upgrade from Starter to Full later?

Yes. Many clients start Starter (or Systems-only Starter), then add an automation lane or Field Ops when the bottleneck is clear.

Where do OutreachEngine, Email-Agent, and Social live?

They are part of Full / Field Ops scope — not sold as vague $497 middle SKUs on the pricing page. See Growth Systems overview, CRM Outreach, and Social systems.

Ticketing & job workflows is the deep product page for field apps and dispatch (already a long-form page). This Growth package page is the commercial ladder and lane chooser.
