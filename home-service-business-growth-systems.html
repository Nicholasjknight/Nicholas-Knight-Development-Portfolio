Lane 1 — Conversion foundation

Estimate-first pages that load on Slow 4G — not brochure sites

Home service buyers search on phones between jobs. Hidden phone numbers, bloated page builders, and generic Services pages lose the call before you quote. We hand-code call-first layouts with service silos, trust signals, and schema — the same foundation whether you are a handyman, painter, or enclosure crew.

  1. Mobile call + form CTAsPhone tap and estimate form visible without scrolling on common device sizes.
  2. Service silosSeparate pages per intent — not one page listing twelve trades you half-do.
  3. After-hours intakeForm submissions route to Email-Agent with auto-acknowledge or push notify options.
  4. Site architecture depthPage structure, silos, and performance patterns are documented on Home Service Websites — this hub wires the full growth stack around that foundation.
Lane 2 — Map + reputation

GBP alignment and reviews where estimate decisions happen

Map-pack visibility and on-page trust are one system. Profile categories and services match site silos; embedded map and review carousel sit beside call CTAs; post-job review asks fire when the job closes — not on a random Tuesday blast.

  1. GBP cleanupCategories, services, hours, and website URL via GBP services.

Lane 3 — Outbound (optional)

CRM outreach when referral-only lead flow plateaus

Not every home service company needs cold email on day one. When property managers, HOAs, and complementary trades become worth pursuing, OutreachEngine adds graded lists, daily send caps, and reply routing into Email-Agent — brand-isolated so your lane never sends as another client's brand.

  1. Graded listsProperty managers and complementary trades scored before first_touch — not scraped blasts.
  2. Send caps20–40 emails per day per lane protects domain reputation.
  3. Reply routingcrm_reply views in Email-Agent — not a buried personal Gmail thread.
  4. When to waitIf you will not answer outreach replies, we do not sell this lane yet. CRM outreach page →

Common questions

How is this different from Home Service Websites?

Home Service Websites covers site architecture, silos, and performance — the website project. This hub is the growth system: website + GBP + reviews as the foundation, with optional CRM, Email-Agent, and referral lanes phased to operator capacity.

How is this different from contractor or excavation growth systems?

Home service lane emphasizes call-first residential trades and repeat homeowner work. Contractor and excavation lanes include heavier portal footprints and wide-geography SEO patterns — different buyer and page-depth models.

Do you build booking calendars?

Yes when estimate flow fits — Knight Group includes booking integration as the reference pattern. Not every trade needs scheduling on day one.

Can you run outreach for us?

We build and configure OutreachEngine; ongoing send operations can be yours or scoped as a managed lane. We will not sell outbound if you will not answer replies.

Which case study should I look at?

Knight Group for full-stack depth, JNS for focused contractor launch, Sal's for search-ready starter. We point you to the closest match on consult — not a dump of every client.
