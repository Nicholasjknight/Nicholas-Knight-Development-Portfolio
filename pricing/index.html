Pricing FAQs

Quick answers for Tampa Bay website pricing, local SEO scope, Google Business work, and deposit options.

How much does a small business website cost in Tampa Bay?

Demo Preview $200, Preview Launch $750 (up to ~20 pages), Local Site $1,200 (up to 35), Authority $2,000 (35–60), Max Authority $4,500 (up to 40 pages PROJECT), Authority Network from $6,500 (60–100+ SCOPED), Growth from $5,000 + $397/mo. Authority and Max include a GBP setup/optimize pass; Maintenance is optional.

Which package is best if I need better Google rankings?

Local Site and Authority Site include search foundation (GSC, schema, analytics). Authority and Max also include GBP setup or optimization. For 60–100+ page networks, see Authority Network (scoped from $6,500). For ongoing map-pack posts, add GBP Maintenance. Deeper ops fit Growth — not thin brochure SKUs.

Can I pay a deposit instead of the full amount?

Yes. Stripe checkout is used for package payments. Larger packages offer deposit-based checkout so you can reserve kickoff first and pay the remaining balance during milestones. Financing may appear in Stripe when eligible.

Do you handle Google Business Profile optimization and schema markup?

Yes. GBP Setup ($497) and Maintenance ($147/mo) checkout under Website Packages. Authority and Max include a setup/optimize pass. Extra schema, citations, or area pages for an existing site are scoped — start with a Growth Audit or Local Visibility consult.

Can I hire Knight Logics for SEO or GBP work on an existing site?

Yes. You do not need a full rebuild first. Existing sites can get GBP work, citations, area pages, and audits — scoped from a Growth Audit or Local Visibility conversation, not a public a-la-carte menu.

What if I need e-commerce, CRM, or automation instead of a brochure site?

Use the e-commerce and growth-system sections as your starting point. Knight Logics also builds storefronts, job record systems, dashboards, CRM-style tools, and workflow automations when a business needs more than a standard website.
