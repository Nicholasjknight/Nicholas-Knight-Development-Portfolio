How The App Works

A straightforward desktop workflow built around reducing wasted processing time.

01

Select input and output

Load a video or still image, choose where the export should land, and define a clip range when you want a quick validation pass on footage.

02

Pick a profile

Choose a speed profile and a content profile so the app sets sane defaults before you go deeper into advanced controls.

03

Preview and estimate

Inspect three real source-frame numbers, choose a fast detail crop or full frame, drag the cyan before/after separator, and cancel or reuse cached preview work.

04

Run the final pass

Launch the full job once settings are proven. DirectML jobs stream decoded frames through AI into the single final encode with one frame resident at a time. Directory-only NCNN and RIFE jobs use disk preflight, bounded batches, checkpoints, and resume.

Packaging

Packaged As A Standalone Windows App

The universal Windows package includes six enhancement engines, RIFE, FFmpeg, and their model assets. Compatible RTX systems receive an automatic first-run setup offer, and a larger NVIDIA edition includes the verified VSR sidecar in the download. The updater stages releases through AppData and relaunches cleanly.

System requirement

A DirectML- or Vulkan-capable Windows GPU is recommended. The NVIDIA profile is only shown on compatible RTX hardware; NVENC is used when available for H.264, HEVC, and AV1 delivery, with software fallbacks.

What it is not

PixelForge AI upscales and optionally resizes in place. It does not crop landscape into vertical Shorts, reframe for TikTok, or force every export to 9:16 unless you explicitly enable final scaling with those dimensions.
