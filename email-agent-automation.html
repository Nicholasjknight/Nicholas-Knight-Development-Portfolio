Lane 1 — crm_reply

OutreachEngine replies surface in a dedicated CRM view

When a prospect answers a first_touch or followup send, Email-Agent classifies the thread as crm_reply — not mixed with newsletters, Formspree intake, or personal mail. Operators triage campaign responses daily without hunting Gmail labels.

  1. Reply detection Inbound mail matched to OutreachEngine send logs — list, variant, and brand lane visible before you draft a response.
  2. CRM Sent folder Outbound campaign mail stays in CRM Sent — separate from manual owner threads and form-lead acknowledgements.

Lane 2 — formspree_lead

Website form fills land in Form Leads — not campaign noise

Formspree webhooks and site contact forms route into formspree_lead views per brand. Estimate requests, consult forms, and referral landing pages stay isolated from OutreachEngine traffic so intake does not get buried under CRM replies.

  1. Webhook + IMAP intake Form submissions create structured queue entries — not a forward-to-personal-Gmail chain nobody monitors.
  2. Brand-mapped routing KL, KG, and ST each get the right company mailbox — Screen Team form leads never appear in Knight Group views.
  3. Optional auto-acknowledge After-hours acknowledgement templates scoped per brand — not one generic “we got your message” for every lane.
Lane 3 — Manual threads

Owner conversations stay operator-owned

Not every email is a campaign reply or a form lead. Manual Sent and manual inbox views keep day-to-day sales conversations, vendor threads, and one-off customer mail out of crm_reply and formspree_lead queues — so morning triage stays fast.

  1. Folder discipline Inbox, Form Leads, CRM Replies, CRM Sent, and Manual Sent mirror how your office actually works — not one undifferentiated stream.
  2. Thread tagging Lead-source attribution when a manual thread started from outreach or a form — without merging queues.
  3. Morning review runbook Unread crm_reply count first, then formspree_lead intake, then manual owner threads — documented for every operator.
Lane 4 — Brand maps

KL / KG / ST credentials and templates stay isolated

Multi-brand Tampa Bay operators cannot afford cross-lane reply mixups. Email-Agent maps Knight Logics, Knight Group, and Screen Team to separate provider credentials — Gmail, Zoho, or Microsoft — with templates and senders that never cross accounts.

  1. Per-lane credentials Each brand connects its own inbox — KG replies do not appear in KL or ST mapped views by accident.
  2. Provider flexibility Gmail, Zoho, and Microsoft supported — wire the accounts your team already uses instead of forcing one provider.
  3. Faith Works lane faithworks maps available where a fourth brand lane is active — same isolation pattern as kl / kg / st.
Lane 5 — Bounce feedback

Bounce signals loop back to OutreachEngine list hygiene

Bad addresses poison domain reputation when they keep receiving capped sends. Email-Agent surfaces bounces and delivery failures to operators and feeds suppression back into OutreachEngine — so the next first_touch window does not hit addresses that already failed.

  1. Operator alerts Failure notifications when a thread or webhook breaks — not silent drops in a shared inbox.
  2. CRM pairing OutreachEngine send logs tie replies and bounces back to list and message variant. CRM outreach case study →
  3. Audit trail Campaign logging connects triage decisions to the outreach lane that started the conversation.

Common questions

How do crm_reply and formspree_lead views differ?

crm_reply is for OutreachEngine campaign responses — replies to first_touch and followup sends. formspree_lead isolates website form intake from campaign and personal traffic. Manual threads stay in separate views so operators never hunt one mixed Gmail inbox.

Which email providers work?

Gmail, Zoho, and Microsoft are supported with per-brand credential mapping for KL, KG, ST, and faithworks lanes where active. Wire the accounts your team already uses — no forced provider swap.

Is Email-Agent only for CRM replies?

No — it also routes Formspree and manual business threads. CRM reply management is one view among several. Website leads and owner conversations get their own queues.

How does bounce handling work?

Bounce and failure signals notify operators and feed back into OutreachEngine so bad addresses stop before the next send window. List hygiene is part of the inbox lane — not a separate spreadsheet task.

How is this different from service-ai-automation?

Business automation covers all production lanes — Knight Command, CRM, email, social, referrals, and dispatch. This page is the dedicated inbox-routing depth: port 5100, view types, brand maps, and bounce loops. AI business automation covers assistive drafts on top of these deterministic rules.

Can I access Email-Agent outside Knight Command?

Port 5100 is reachable directly; Knight Command embeds it for single-sign-on operator workflow. Same routing rules either way.

Who is this for?

Multi-brand Tampa Bay operators drowning in mixed inboxes — especially teams already running OutreachEngine who lose campaign replies in personal Gmail.
