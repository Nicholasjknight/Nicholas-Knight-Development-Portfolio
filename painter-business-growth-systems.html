Lane 1 — Service architecture

Cabinet refinishing and exterior stucco are not the same search

Painting companies compete on visual proof and intent match. Harbor-line demo imagery shows how separate silos pair job photos with estimate CTAs — the pattern we recruit into the open painting lane.

Lane 2 — Portfolio proof

Before/after galleries belong beside the estimate button

Homeowners choose painters on visual confidence. Proof buried on a separate gallery page loses the visitor still comparing three map-pack results on a phone.

  1. Per-intent galleries Interior, exterior, and cabinet jobs paired with the service silo that earned the click — not a mixed Instagram grid.
  2. Scope captions Surface prep, product lines, and timeline context — answers “can they handle my job?” without a phone call.
  3. Reviews near CTAs Google review highlights and FAQ blocks on the same viewport as estimate forms.
Lane 3 — Search-ready foundation

GA4, Clarity, GSC, and schema from launch — not after ads spend

Common questions

Is this a full 97-page build like Knight Group?

No — painting scope scales to your metros and service depth. Sal's shows search-ready starter; deep geography and outreach follow when justified by coverage and volume.

Can handyman or remodel partners refer painting work?

Yes — referral infrastructure attributes consults to the sending partner when both are in the network. Painting partners typically receive finish work from handyman, drywall, and remodeling trades.

Do you handle brand design?

Sal's included a brand package in scope — logo, color, and hero direction. Similar deliverables can bundle per project when you do not have production-ready marks.

How do cabinet and exterior pages differ?

They target different search intents and gallery proof. Cabinet pages emphasize kitchen/bath refinishing; exterior pages emphasize prep, coatings, and Gulf Coast durability — separate URLs, separate FAQs.

When should CRM outreach start?

After the site and GBP produce steady estimate flow and someone will answer crm_reply queues — often property managers and remodelers for commercial paint scopes.

What if I only have phone photos?

We scope gallery quality in intake — some partners launch with a focused set of approved jobs and expand galleries as new work completes. Proof quality matters more than volume on day one.
