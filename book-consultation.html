Which consultation matches your business

The right starting point depends on what is actually broken today — not on picking the biggest-sounding option.

Local trades and service businesses

If you are not sure whether the problem is your website, your Google Business Profile, or both, start with the free Website + GBP Audit. It is built to diagnose before recommending a fix — not to sell a package before understanding the problem.

Owners planning a new build or rebuild

The Website Strategy Call fits businesses that already know they need a new site or a rebuild but want scope, structure, and platform ownership clarified before committing to a package tier.

Businesses with a site that isn't ranking

Local SEO + GBP Strategy is for owners with an existing website who need a concrete 30-day plan for rankings, maps visibility, and technical fixes — not a vague promise to "do SEO."

Teams drowning in manual work

The Automation Discovery Call fits operators buried in repetitive admin, disconnected spreadsheets, or manual ticket-to-invoice work who need a realistic automation scope before any code gets written.

Consultation FAQs

Everything you need to know before you request a time.

What if I am not sure which consultation type fits?

Choose the custom-fit option or the free Website and GBP Audit. Knight Logics can still route the request to the right next step after reviewing the business context.

Is the Website and GBP Audit really free?

Yes. It is meant to help local businesses understand where their current visibility and website issues are actually coming from before committing to a larger scope.

Do I need a finished scope before I request a call?

No. Rough direction is enough. If you know what is broken, what you want to improve, or what kind of business outcome you need, that is enough to have a useful consultation.

Can businesses outside Tampa Bay use this page?

Yes. Knight Logics targets Tampa Bay locally, but remote consultations are available for businesses anywhere. The local focus improves discoverability, not who is allowed to book.

Will I be pressured into a package on the call?

No. Each consultation is scoped to answer a specific question — what's broken, what a rebuild should look like, what SEO priorities matter, or what automation is realistic. Package recommendations only follow once the underlying problem is clear.

What if my need spans more than one consultation type?

Pick the closest match and mention the overlap in the project details field. Most real businesses need some mix of website, visibility, and systems work — the call adapts once we understand your actual priorities.

Can I book directly instead of filling out the form?

If timing is urgent, call or text (813) 773-5553 directly. The form exists so the reply already has enough context to make the call useful instead of a cold intro.
