Step 1 — Foundation

Registration, email, phone, and cards first

Before public marketing, the business should look and operate like a business — offline contacts and brand footing first, not a template homepage with a personal Gmail.

  1. Entity & insurance footing Name clarity, Sunbiz / registration path, and insurance readiness where your trade requires it — so you are not marketing work you cannot legally take.
  2. Domain + business email Company addresses on your domain (not @gmail forever). In most cases there is no monthly Knight Logics email fee — we set you up on a standard provider plan you own (often free or low-cost).
  3. Business phone line A number quoted on cards, GBP, website, and forms — tracked and owned by the company, not whoever happens to answer their personal cell.
  4. Logo + print-ready brand mark A clean mark that works on cards, signs, and decals — designed for production files, not just a social avatar.
  5. Business cards Print-ready fronts/backs with NAP, website, and services — we generate the art and route the cheapest durable print option (typically Amazon / online print).
  6. Optional field kit add-ons Yard signs, banners, and vehicle decals use the same art pipeline when you want trucks and job sites advertising the brand.

Common questions

What comes first — the website or registration?

Foundation first. We want entity/registration clarity and insurance where your trade requires it, then business email on your domain, a business phone line, and cards — so every public surface quotes company contacts, not a personal Gmail and cell. Google Business Profile and the website come next once name, phone, and service area are real. Optional yard signs, banners, and vehicle decals can follow. CRM, social, field ops, and review systems are growth lanes after that footing is solid — not day-one requirements.

How much does a website cost for a new business?

It depends on how far you need to go on day one. Packages typically run from Demo Preview and Preview Launch through Local Site, Authority Site, Max Authority, Authority Network, and full Growth System (setup + monthly). A consult maps what you already have (LLC, insurance, logo, phone) versus what still needs to be built, then we give a written estimate for that scope — not a surprise invoice after go-live. See pricing for current package ranges, or book a consult if you want a phased plan.

When do we make the GBP vs the website live?

Claim and verify Google Business Profile as soon as the business name, service area, and phone are real — often while the site is still on staging. That gets you into Maps early. Publish the website only after package scope is locked and it passes Website Audit (indexability, SEO, GEO, and AEO readiness — not just a pretty homepage). After DNS is public, add the website URL on GBP and keep name, address, phone, hours, and categories identical on both. Mismatched NAP is one of the fastest ways to look unfinished online.

Do you help with yard signs, banners, or vehicle decals?

Yes — those are optional brand deliverables, not required for launch. Many trades want job-site and truck visibility once the digital brand is set. We design yard signs, banners, and car/truck decals to match the same logo, phone, and website URL you just launched, so offline and online marketing do not look like two different companies.

Do you offer lead gen, outreach, social, and field ops?

Yes — as separate growth-system lanes after the foundation is live. CRM outreach and lead grading, Email-Agent inbox routing, social posting automation, ticketing/invoicing/field dispatch, referral tracking, and review requests are all productized. You can add one lane or several; we will not force a full stack on a brand that is still filing paperwork or soft-launching.

How is this different from Business Growth Systems?

This page is the sequenced start path for new and restarting businesses: registration → contacts → GBP/website/audits → optional field brand → growth lanes. Business Growth Systems is the flagship hub that demonstrates the live stack — outreach, email, referrals, social, and dispatch — and links into each lane. Most new owners start here; established companies that already have a site often go straight to Growth Systems or a free growth audit.

What if I already have a DIY site or social pages?

That is common. We audit what you already have — domain, email, phone, GBP, and the current site — then decide what to keep, rebuild, or replace. Soft-launched DIY sites often need NAP cleanup, indexability fixes, and a clearer conversion path before we add outreach or automation. Bring what you have; the plan starts from reality, not a blank slate.

How long does a new-business launch take?

Foundation and contacts can move in days once decisions are made. A Search-Ready or Local Site website is typically weeks on staging with an audit gate before public DNS. Growth-system lanes (outreach, social, dispatch) follow in milestones so your team is not trained on five tools on day one. Timeline depends on how ready registration, insurance, brand assets, and content are when we start.
