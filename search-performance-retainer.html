Why click bands instead of hours

Most “SEO retainers” bill time whether or not Search Console moves. This lane prices ongoing technical and content work against documented Google Search Console Web clicks on your property — the same commercial language used on live contractor Performance Order Forms.

You get a written Original Baseline, measurement periods, and published fee bands. When clicks rise into a higher band, the monthly fee true-ups. When the first measurement quarter stays at or below baseline, the form can true-up to $0 for that quarter. Maintenance floor after anniversary and annual rebase rules live in the signed order — not as marketing vagueness.

This is not a promise that every query will rank #1. It is an operated search lane with a measurement contract both sides can audit in GSC. Paid ads, brand Direct traffic, and unrelated properties are out of scope unless the form explicitly expands the property list.

How we work the property

Intake confirms Search Console access, the property URL set, and whether Google Business Profile work is already covered by Maintenance or a project GBP pass. We prioritize pages and queries that can realistically convert for your trade — not vanity impressions on informational terms that never call.

Typical work includes fixing crawl and index issues, strengthening service and city architecture, improving titles/meta/H1 alignment for CTR, adding FAQ and schema where it helps, and iterating content that already ranks in positions 4–20. Search Performance FAQs

Is this Instant Package checkout?

No. Retainers use signed order forms and invoicing. Instant Packages are fixed one-time digital deliverables.

Where do the numbers come from?

Commercial language tracks contractor Performance Order Forms under Contracts/clients (Roof Monsters RM-PERF path and equivalents).

Which GSC search type counts?

Web clicks on the agreed property, rolling 90 days, unless the signed form expands scope.

What if clicks drop after a Google update?

True-up rules, floors, and rebase language in the order form govern fee changes. We do not invent a verbal override on this page.

Do you need Search Console access?

Yes. Without GSC, there is no auditable baseline or band measurement.

Can this run with Growth Systems?

Yes as separate lanes. Search fees and CRM/% partnership fees stay on distinct forms so the same outcome is not billed twice.

How long until bands move?

Organic work compounds over measurement quarters. First-quarter true-up to $0 exists specifically for properties that start at baseline.

Do you offer this to non-contractor businesses?

The published band language grew out of contractor Performance Order Forms, but the same click-band mechanics can apply to other local service businesses. Bring your Search Console history to a consult and we will tell you honestly whether the model fits.

What happens at intake before the first invoice?

We confirm Search Console access, document your Original Baseline in writing, and set the property scope on the signed order form before any band applies — nothing is billed against an undocumented starting point.
