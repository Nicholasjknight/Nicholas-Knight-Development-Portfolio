How attribution stays honest

Partnership jobs are those sourced through channels named on the form: website and GBP paths we operate, outreach leads we send, social campaigns in scope, and referrals from our partner network. Your preexisting book of business stays in the “your jobs” bucket unless the form explicitly reclassifies a channel.

When a homeowner from an original Knight Logics lead books again inside the referral window, that repeat work typically remains partnership for the period written on the form. That protects both sides from “we only pay on the first ticket” games while still leaving your independent jobs untouched.

Why net, not gross

A percentage of gross revenue punishes you for taking on a job with high material costs — a $10,000 roof replacement with $6,000 in shingles and permits is not the same win as a $10,000 job that costs $1,500 to deliver. Pricing our fee against net, after direct job costs and an agreed overhead percentage, means the incentive stays aligned: we only get paid well on jobs that were actually profitable for you too. It also means we have no reason to push you toward high-revenue, low-margin work just to inflate our own fee.

The overhead percentage exists because "direct costs" alone would ignore real business expense — insurance, vehicles, office staff, software — that a job still has to absorb before anything is truly profit. Capping that overhead share at 30% of customer paid keeps the formula from being gamed in either direction: high enough to be fair to your fixed costs, capped low enough that it cannot be used to manufacture a near-zero net on every job.

Trade Partnership FAQs

Is Search Performance the same product?

No. Search Performance is GSC click-band monthly fees. Partnership % applies to attributed jobs from growth channels. Order forms keep lanes separate to avoid double billing.

Can we use 8% instead of 10%?

Commercial rate is negotiated on the signed form within the published 8–10% band for net on partnership jobs.

What if costs are late?

If costs are not listed within 14 days, fallback fees in the signed form apply (for example a % of customer paid on B1-style terms).

Do you take a cut of my whole company?

No. Your jobs stay 100% yours. We only fee partnership jobs after the net formula.

Is the app optional?

No for partnership attribution. Without logging, there is no auditable net.

How do Faith Works B1/B2 terms relate?

Public marketing tracks those Option B proposal patterns (simple overhead % vs equipment-hours variants). Your signed form is the binding document.

Can this coexist with a monthly Growth Systems fee?

Yes when the order forms define which outcomes are covered by monthly ops versus % of net so the same job is not billed twice.

What counts as a "direct cost" versus overhead?

Direct costs are job-specific — materials, fuel for that job, subcontractors, permits, and job-specific repairs. Overhead is your general cost of doing business (insurance, office, vehicles, software) applied as an agreed percentage rather than itemized per job. The exact line-item rules are written into your signed order form.

Who decides whether a job counts as "partnership"?

The order form names the qualifying channels — our website/GBP paths, outreach leads, in-scope social campaigns, and partner referrals. Jobs sourced entirely through your own preexisting book of business are not partnership jobs unless the form says otherwise.
