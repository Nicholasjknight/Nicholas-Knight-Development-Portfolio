Fulfillment and quality gates

Each Instant SKU runs an automated worker after Stripe checkout. Outside-in health packages crawl up to five public pages with Playwright, axe-core, and mobile Lighthouse, then attach public signal checks (robots, sitemap, llms.txt, schema, headers). Quality gates must pass or the system retries; after three gate failures the order auto-refunds. Full-Access verifies Search Console permission for audits@knightlogics.com before the GSC API pack runs, then bundles the health PDF.

Samples stay available for proof but are noindexed so they do not compete as thin SEO pages. Parent product pages remain the indexable commercial surfaces. Download links expire after 30 days — save the PDF when you receive it.
