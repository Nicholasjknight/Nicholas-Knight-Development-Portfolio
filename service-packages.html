Common questions

Can I buy from the hub or only product pages?

Both. Hub Buy buttons and product-page Buy buttons use the same Stripe SKUs via instant-package-checkout.js.

How is Instant different from Search Performance Retainer?

Instant is one-time automated evidence with fixed scope and fixed price. Search Performance is ongoing human operator work — monthly GSC click-band analysis, competitive research, and implementation. Buy Instant to document baseline; buy Mode B when you need someone operating search monthly.

Do Instant Packages include a website build?

No. They produce PDF or CSV evidence. Website packages, GBP management, and Growth Systems are separate products with separate scope.

Are samples indexed?

Sample pages are publicly reachable for proof but noindexed so they do not dilute the paid product pages.

What if my site blocks bots?

Outside-in crawls need publicly reachable pages. Login walls, aggressive bot blocks, or geo firewalls can prevent a valid run — gates fail and refunds apply when evidence cannot be produced.

Is white-label allowed for resale?

Yes for the $49 white-label SKU: same engine, your agency name on the cover. Client ownership and your capacity rules still apply — see the white-label product page.

What is the difference between Health ($39) and AI Search Readiness ($79)?

Health is the broad outside-in crawl (Playwright, axe, Lighthouse, plus public signals). AI Search Readiness is a specialized GEO/AEO module focused on llms.txt, FAQPage, AI crawler policy, and related schema/robots signals — not the full multi-tool health PDF.

Does Conversion Leak Audit guarantee more leads?

No. It documents form, CTA, phone, and trust-cue findings from a Playwright scan. Fixing leaks and CRM work stay separate.

What GSC permission does Full-Access need?

Full permission for audits@knightlogics.com on the property you specify at checkout. We verify before the API pack runs; if access cannot be confirmed within 14 days, the order refunds.
