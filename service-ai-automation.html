Common questions

How much does business automation cost in Tampa Bay?

One-Time Automation Build is scoped from $500–$10,000+: simple workflows are typically $500–$1,500, integrated builds are $1,500–$5,000, and custom builds are $5,000–$10,000+. Ongoing managed Growth Systems are priced separately as setup plus monthly service.

Is this the same as “AI automation”?

This page covers production ops lanes — Knight Command, OutreachEngine, Email-Agent, social queues, referrals, and optional dispatch. Assistive AI drafts and triage live on AI business automation with deterministic caps and brand maps underneath.

What systems can you connect or automate together?

CRMs, forms, email platforms, Google Workspace, spreadsheets, payment systems, supplier feeds, internal dashboards, and third-party APIs — scoped around tools you already use instead of forcing a platform change first.

How long does an automation project usually take?

A focused single workflow can often be scoped, built, and tested in one to two weeks. Larger systems with multiple APIs, dashboard interfaces, or Knight Command embeds take longer. Clear workflow scope before build-out is the biggest time saver.

What kinds of tasks can actually be automated?

Repetitive data entry, report generation, lead routing, email follow-up, invoice processing, file organization, API-to-spreadsheet syncs, scheduled alerts — if your team does the same thing manually more than a few times a week, it is a candidate.

Do I need a developer on staff to maintain it after launch?

No. Every automation ships with documentation and, where practical, configurable settings you can adjust yourself. For complex systems, a periodic maintenance check keeps everything running without a full-time hire.

What tools do you build automations with?

Python scripts, REST APIs, Google Workspace, webhook pipelines, cron/Task Scheduler jobs, Flask services, and custom-built systems depending on what fits. The right tool is whatever runs reliably — not whatever is trendy.

Do you work with businesses outside Tampa Bay?

Yes. Tampa Bay is the primary local service area, but automation projects can be delivered remotely. Local targeting helps regional discovery; it does not limit who can hire Knight Logics.

What is Knight Command vs a one-off script?

Knight Command is the authenticated operator shell that embeds live growth-system services in one login. One-off scripts solve a single workflow; Knight Command is for teams running outreach, email, social, and referrals daily across brands.
