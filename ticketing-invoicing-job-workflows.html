Closeout Email summary + PDF packet

What the property manager actually receives

When that portal client’s week closes in Handyman Ticket Manager, one HTML email goes out with the job table and Stripe pay buttons — plus a compressed service-report PDF. One CRM reply’s worth of relationship; a full week of billable work in one packet.

  • Email body: ticket #, property, work summary, visit/completion meta, labor · materials · totals, then one Pay link per property
  • PDF cover: weekly totals table (ticket, property, work, completed, labor, materials markup, total)
  • PDF per job: WO title, property/unit, work performed, visit details, technician, amounts, completion notes, before/after photos
  • Photos auto-compressed so the PDF stays under Graph’s ~3.5 MB send limit
