Case study breakdown

What changed, what shipped, and what this lane proves for other demolition and land-service companies.

Problem

A legacy WordPress footprint mixed blog posts, thin service pages, and outdated URL paths. Buyers looking for mobile home demolition, shed removal, or stump excavation needed clear silos, local coverage, job proof, and a fast way to request an estimate — without a page-builder stack that buries call CTAs on mobile.

  • Demolition intent competed with generic land-service messaging
  • Legacy post URLs needed redirects into durable project hubs
  • Area coverage needed honest radius documentation across Central Florida
  • Lead capture needed call-or-text + estimate paths that match field ops

What we built

A full hand-coded site on breakinggroundlsad.com with demolition-first information architecture, land & trees support services, project galleries, service-area hubs, pricing/policy pages, and estimate-first CTAs.

  • 79 indexable URLs with sitemap and robots hygiene for Search Console launch
  • Core hubs: demolition, mobile home demolition, shed/barn removal, land clearing, tree removal, stump removal, grading, pond drainage, storm debris
  • 50+ area pages under /areas/ plus service-area index
  • Project proof pages for clearing, stump, pond, shed, and hurricane cleanup jobs
  • Preserved canonicals + redirect map for WordPress cutover without SEO loss
  • Schema, llms.txt, and LocalBusiness entity data aligned to Kathleen / Polk NAP

Positioning that matches the business

Public messaging leads with mobile home and light-structure demolition — the high-intent, high-ticket jobs that define the brand — then supports land clearing and site work for the same property owners. Father-and-son ownership and equipment readiness are visible without overclaiming company age.
