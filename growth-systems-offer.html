How intake and onboarding work

Intake checklist

  1. Primary trade, service area, and capacity for new jobs
  2. Website + Google Business Profile access (or launch status)
  3. Whether CRM replies are closed by your team or ours
  4. Which channels count as partnership jobs for % terms
  5. Brand lanes and sender accounts that must stay separated
  6. Weekly review window and escalation contacts

Downloadable order-form language is provided after consult. For percent partnerships with app requirements, see Trade Partnership.

Onboarding maps your niches and geos into graded OutreachEngine lists, wires form and CRM replies into Email-Agent inboxes, and — when included — stands up social queues and referral codes so attribution is measurable from day one. We do not promise a fixed number of booked jobs; we promise operated systems with visible queues, caps, and failure states.

If your website still cannot convert a cold visitor, Growth Systems will amplify a weak front door. In that case we recommend finishing a Local or Authority website project first (see pricing), then attaching Systems-only Growth when leads are dying in the inbox instead of on the SERP.

Growth Systems FAQs

Is this a downloadable SaaS CRM?

No. Outreach CRM is operated as a service for your business. We are not selling the CRM as a self-serve product in this phase.

How is this different from Instant Packages?

Instant Packages are fixed digital deliverables with Stripe checkout. Growth Systems are ongoing, you-run retainers with signed commercial terms.

Do you guarantee booked jobs?

No. We guarantee operated systems with caps, grading, routing, and weekly review. Job volume depends on your capacity, offer, geography, and reply speed.

Can we start Systems-only if we already have a site?

Yes when the front door converts. If the site is the bottleneck, we will say so in intake and point you to the website packages first.

How does this interact with Trade Partnership %?

When % of net applies, partnership jobs must be logged in the app. Search click fees stay on the Search Performance form so the same revenue is not billed twice.

What happens if a send or social post fails?

Failures surface in the queue and Logs tab during weekly review — not a silent gap nobody notices for a week.

Can we cancel?

Cancellation, notice periods, and true-up rules are in the signed order form — not on this marketing page alone.
