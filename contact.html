Who typically reaches out

Most inquiries fall into one of a few patterns — recognizing yours helps you write a message that gets a faster, more useful reply.

"My site looks dated or is slow"

Owners running an old template, a bloated builder, or a site that has not been touched in years. Mention your current platform and what feels broken — that alone is enough to start a rebuild conversation.

"I'm not showing up on Google"

Businesses that rank poorly in Maps or organic search despite doing good work. Include your city, your main service, and whether your Google Business Profile is claimed and active.

"I need a store, not just a brochure site"

Product or service businesses ready to sell online. Mention product count, whether pricing is fixed or custom-quoted, and any platform you have already tried.

"We're drowning in manual work"

Operators juggling spreadsheets, disconnected tools, or repetitive ticket-to-invoice work. Describe the actual workflow step that eats the most time — that is usually the highest-value automation target.

Contact FAQs

Quick answers before you send the brief or request a consultation.

Should I use this page or the booking page?

Use this page when you want to send business details first or ask a broader question. Use the booking page when you already want a call and need to request a specific consultation type and preferred time.

How fast will Knight Logics reply?

Most contact requests get a direct response within 24 hours. If timing is urgent, calling or texting is the fastest option.

What should I include in my message?

Include what your business does, what is not working now, what you want to improve, and any timeline or budget you already know. If you have an existing website or Google Business Profile, include that too.

Do you only work with Tampa Bay businesses?

Tampa Bay is the strongest local focus for web design, local SEO, and Google Business Profile work, but remote website, e-commerce, and automation projects are still possible outside the area.

Can I ask about GBP or automation without a full rebuild?

Yes. Local SEO cleanup, Google Business Profile work, audits, automation planning, and internal-tool projects can all be scoped as standalone engagements.

Do you reply to every message, even small ones?

Yes. Every submitted form gets a reply within 24 hours, even if the answer is that the request is outside current scope or better suited to a different service.

What if I already got a quote from a package page?

Mention the package name in the project details field. The form pre-fills relevant context when you arrive from a package page, so the reply can confirm scope instead of starting over from scratch.
