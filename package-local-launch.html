Who Local Site is for — and when to choose something else

Good fit

  • Local trades and service businesses ready for a real domain and professional site
  • Owners who need up to ~35 pages of services, areas, and proof
  • Teams that want search foundation without buying a Growth ops stack yet
  • Businesses that may add GBP later instead of bundling it blindly

Choose a different package

  • Still validating? Demo Preview ($200) or Preview Launch ($750)
  • Need 35–60 pages + GBP pass included? Authority Site ($2,000)
  • Need deeper authority? Max Authority ($4,500 · up to 40 pages) or Authority Network (from $6,500)
  • Need lead ops / field app / automation? Growth System (Website+Systems or Systems-only)

Need more pages later? Upgrade paths to Authority or Max are normal. Local Site is the durable middle of the ladder — not a disposable brochure.

What “search foundation” means here

On Local Site, search foundation is the launch wiring that makes a domain-backed site eligible to compete — not a promise that you will rank #1 next week. Concretely: Search Console property verification, sitemap submission readiness, core LocalBusiness / service-oriented schema where it fits, and analytics so you can see whether traffic and form fills are real. It does not include a 25–75 citation blitz, weekly content factory, or paid ads management. Those are separate scoped engagements if you need them later.

Page count up to 35 is a ceiling for this SKU, not a mandate to invent thin pages. Better a tight 18–25 page site that matches how you actually sell than 35 hollow city clones. During intake we map services and areas to pages that earn their keep — homepage, primary services, key service areas, gallery or project proof, FAQ, about, contact, and supporting conversion pages as needed.

If your market truly needs dozens of city × service combinations on day one, say so early. That is the signal to step up to Authority (35–60), Max Authority (up to 40 deep PROJECT pages), or Authority Network (60–100+ SCOPED from $6,500) — or to pair a site ladder package with a Growth Systems-only lane when ops — not pages — is the bottleneck.

After launch — what success looks like

Your domain loads on HTTPS. Forms reach an inbox you check. Search Console is connected. The site presents services and areas clearly on phone and desktop. You are not stuck in a page-builder subscription. From there, growth is optional: GBP Maintenance for maps cadence, Monthly Plans for edits and posts, or Growth Systems-only if lead follow-up is the real leak.

Local Site FAQs

Is GBP included?

No. Local Site keeps GBP optional. Authority and Max include a setup or optimization pass because deeper local authority usually needs maps alignment.

Do I own the code?

Yes — custom-coded HTML/CSS/JS for your project, not a locked page-builder tenancy. Hosting and domain registrar fees remain yours.

What about monthly care?

Local Site is a project. After the minor-fix window, ongoing edits and GBP posts live under Monthly Plans or GBP Maintenance.

Can I add a quote form or lead tracker later?

Yes. Standard contact form is included. Stronger intake is Lead Capture Upgrade. Full CRM/ops is Growth.

Is this the same as Growth System Starter?

No. Growth Starter includes a much deeper site plus lead tracker, review sequences, reporting, and monthly care — or Systems-only if you already have a site.
