How OutreachEngine works

Crawlable steps from list build to booked work

Brand-lane templates go out through OutreachEngine; Email-Agent shows CRM Sent threads and routes replies. Screen Team st template + Faith Works faithworks mailbox below — same pipeline for contractor and property-manager lists.

  1. Build and tag lists by trade, metro, and ICP — bounce-suppress known bad addresses before first send.
  2. Preview templates per brand lane (kl/kg/st/faithworks) so sender identity never crosses brands.
  3. Scheduler fires first_touch then followup under 20–40 daily send caps with bounce flags.
  4. Replies route into Email-Agent crm_reply views; operators triage and book estimates from tracked threads.
  5. Lead-source reporting shows which list and message variant produced replies and booked work.

CRM replies, form leads, and brand mailboxes — not personal Gmail threads

Email-Agent on port 5100 is the CRM email integration layer behind OutreachEngine. Website contact form leads, Formspree submissions, and campaign replies route into brand-mapped mailboxes for Knight Logics, Knight Group, Screen Team, Faith Works, and Roof Monsters — so inbound lead routing never lands in a personal inbox.

Operators triage from dedicated folders: Inbox, Form Leads, CRM Replies, CRM Sent, and Manual Sent. Critical replies and Needs review counters surface hot threads before the morning gets buried in newsletters and spam.

Common questions

How does OutreachEngine work end to end?

Lists are imported and tagged, templates previewed per brand, scheduler jobs send first_touch and followup under daily caps, bounces are suppressed, and replies land in Email-Agent crm_reply for operator triage into booked work.

What daily send volume do you recommend?

We typically cap between 20 and 40 sends per day per brand while warming domains and monitoring bounce rates — enough for outbound lead generation without torching reputation.

Can this support property manager outreach?

Yes — property manager and complementary-trade segments are first-class list tags with dedicated templates and caps separate from consumer blast tools.

Can this connect to my existing inbox?

Yes — Email-Agent on port 5100 routes CRM replies into crm_reply views separate from manual conversations and Formspree leads.

Is OutreachEngine included or custom?

We deploy and configure OutreachEngine for your brands, lists, and follow-up rules — adapted from our live KL/KG/ST/faithworks workflows.

Who is CRM outreach for?

Estimate-first Tampa Bay trades and multi-brand operators who need contractor email outreach with send caps and reply routing — not one-off newsletter blasts.
