The problem

Restaurant and retail sites stall at PDF menus and manual orders

Hospitality and product businesses outgrow brochure sites — but full POS platforms are expensive and rigid. Owners need ordering, events, and editable content without rebuilding every year. The middle ground between "static PDF menu" and "enterprise POS contract" is where most Tampa Bay hospitality and retail brands actually need to launch.

  • Menu changes require a developer for every special
  • Events and hours are buried or outdated
  • Checkout is phone-only while competitors take online orders
  • No room to add Toast or POS integrations later
  • Product catalogs outgrow social-only selling but are not ready for Shopify overhead
