Preview Launch FAQs

Is this live on my domain?

Typically no — Preview Launch is a hosted preview for proof-of-concept. Domain + search foundation arrive with Local Site and above.

How is this different from Demo Preview?

More pages, stronger multi-page structure, and a higher bar for sales demos. Demo is the $200 validation stub; Preview is the $750 multi-page proof.

Can I go straight to Local instead?

Yes. If you already know you need a domain and search foundation, Local Site is the better buy. Preview is for when you still need to prove shape before launch spend.

Does credit apply upward?

Ask at checkout — Preview work is designed to roll into a full launch package when you upgrade.

Who is Preview Launch for?

Owners who need a multi-page proof for sales conversations, investor/partner buy-in, or internal alignment before paying for domain launch and search foundation. It is also useful when Demo Preview felt too thin but Local Site still feels early.

What does “up to 10 pages” mean?

It is the fixed scope ceiling for this tier — homepage, services, areas, about, FAQ, contact, and supporting preview pages as needed. We do not invent empty pages just to hit ten.

How Preview Launch fits the ladder

Demo Preview ($200) proves a vibe. Preview Launch ($750) proves a site shape. Local Site ($1,200) puts that shape on your domain with search foundation. Authority and Max deepen page networks and include a GBP pass. Growth Systems add lead ops, follow-up, and optional field apps — with Systems-only pricing when you already have a usable site.

If your only goal is “get found on Google Maps this quarter,” Preview Launch is the wrong SKU. Pair a live site package with GBP Setup/Maintenance, or jump to Authority if you need maps alignment with deeper pages immediately.

Expect the preview to feel like a real multi-page business site: navigation that makes sense, services that are not crammed into one scroll, areas that acknowledge where you work, and a clear estimate or contact path. That is what stakeholders need to say yes to a larger launch budget.

When you upgrade, we are not starting from a napkin sketch. The Preview structure becomes the blueprint for Local or Authority — which is why this tier exists instead of forcing everyone straight from a $200 stub into a $1,200–$4,500 build.

Preview Launch is the honest middle for owners who need more than a stub and less than a full local SEO launch. It is custom-coded, multi-page, and upgrade-aware. It is not indexed marketing theater and it is not a Growth System. Use it to de-risk the next spend — then move to Local, Authority, Max, or Growth with a structure that already looks like your business.
