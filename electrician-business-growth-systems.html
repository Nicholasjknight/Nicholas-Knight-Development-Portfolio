Lane 1 — Service architecture

Panel upgrades, EV chargers, and commercial bids rank on different searches

Homeowners search “electrician near me” with different urgency than property managers pricing tenant improvements or HOAs planning EV infrastructure. Thin templates hide those intents and lose clicks to competitors with clearer offers.

  1. Residential silos Panel upgrades, rewiring, lighting, surge protection, and whole-home electrical — each with scope clarity and estimate CTAs above the fold.
  2. Commercial & tenant improvement Separate pages for office build-outs, retail fit-ups, and recurring maintenance contracts — not buried under residential copy.
  3. Emergency vs scheduled After-hours and storm-response messaging distinct from bid-request flows — so GBP categories and website services stay aligned.
  4. EV charger installs Dedicated intent for Gulf Coast EV adoption — Gulf-Pinellas demo imagery shows how charger and panel-upgrade proof pairs on-page.

Common questions

Is this only for large electrical companies?

No — Farrell Electric shows the starter path for owner-operators. Full growth systems scale with your metros, commercial volume, and outreach readiness — not a minimum page count.

How does the referral network work for electricians?

After a credible owned presence and case study, you get a tracked /ref/:partner lane and dashboard visibility. Handyman, HVAC, and GC partners in the roster send work with attribution — earned vs paid status is documented, not argued in spreadsheets.

Do you compete with my existing marketing agency?

We build your owned presence, technical SEO foundation, and network slot — you keep your brand domain and customer relationships. Ads and traditional agencies can run on top once the site and GBP convert.

When should CRM outreach come online?

When builder, property manager, or GC lists justify daily send caps and someone on your team will answer crm_reply queues. Website and referrals often come first for new lane partners.

Can you separate emergency from commercial messaging?

Yes — that is core to the silo architecture. Emergency pages carry after-hours CTAs and storm-response FAQs; commercial pages target bid requests and recurring maintenance — different intents, different URLs.
