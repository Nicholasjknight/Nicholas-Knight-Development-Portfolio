Lane 4 — Safe boundaries

Where AI helps vs where rules engines own the send

Generic AI demos hallucinate on job details and bypass approval steps. Useful business automation needs guardrails: AI drafts and sorts inside queues humans already open; OutreachEngine caps, Email-Agent brand maps, bounce suppression, and customer-facing replies stay rule-backed with explicit approval where brand risk is high.

  1. Assistive, not autonomous High-risk outbound and customer replies require human approval — AI proposes, operators send.
  2. No unsupervised chatbots Customer-facing AI scoped only with logging, escalation paths, and explicit guardrails.
  3. Visible failures Broken automations surface in Logs tabs — not discovered when customers complain.
