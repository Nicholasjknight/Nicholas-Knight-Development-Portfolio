Lane 1 — Field capture

Before / process / after — tied to job ID on site

KG Dispatch (Handyman Ticket Manager) requires scoped uploads per ticket — not a camera-roll dump after the truck leaves. Browser-first mobile capture keeps crews off another app store install while timestamps and captions stay on the job record permanently.

  1. Status-gated closeout Job cannot finish until required photo scopes are attached — office stops chasing texts for “any pics?”
  2. Per-scope labels Before, repair process, and after sections match what property managers reject when proof is vague.
  3. Cross-trade examples Handyman AC filter jobs, pool enclosure screen work, and land-clearing before/after — same pipeline, different templates.
Lane 2 — Branded composites

One upload becomes marketing frames — not three manual crops

A background worker builds before | process | after composites with brand headers, service labels, and geography — the Knight Group AC filter pattern. Composites feed the website gallery, social-ready JPGs for the kg Social Poster queue, and compressed pages inside service-report PDFs.

  1. Template once Brand frame, fonts, and section layout encoded per client — crews never rebuild Canva after every job.
  2. Auto-compress for email PDF photo pages sized under Graph send limits when reports ride with invoice emails.
  3. Redaction posture Client PII stripped from public gallery composites; job ID stays in ops views.
Lane 3 — Website gallery

Completed work becomes owned proof content — without re-upload

Composites publish to client gallery pages with schema-friendly alt text and city/service tags — Screen Team pool enclosures, Knight Group handyman jobs, Faith Works land clearing. Marketing proof compounds from the same capture path crews already use to close tickets.

  1. Gallery worker Approved composites push to the site gallery with captions pulled from job metadata.
  2. Panoramic and detail shots Wide job photography (pool enclosures, large sites) complements before/after composites on gallery grids.
  3. Social handoff Social-ready JPGs fan into brand queues — see Social Media Automation for publish runners.
Lane 4 — PDF service reports

Portal-ready packets — cover totals plus per-job photo pages

Server-side PDF generation runs branded layouts per property manager requirements: weekly cover table, then per-job sections with WO title, unit, technician, amounts, completion notes, and scoped photos. Office reviews the packet before billing fires when proof-gating is configured.

  1. Manager format matching Templates encode required sections once — portal rejections stop eating Friday afternoons.
  2. Land clearing before/after Visual trades (forestry mulching, stump removal) use paired proof sections managers expect on heavy equipment jobs.
  3. Download + email PDF exports for portal upload, customer links, or attachment to weekly vendor email.
Lane 5 — Invoice packet

Reports ride with invoices — proof before pay asks

Weekly Vendoroo closeout emails attach the compressed service-report PDF beside Stripe pay links — managers see job tables, photo proof, and per-property pay buttons in one send. This is the handoff to Stripe Invoice Automation; photo PDFs are not optional decoration when portals audit completion.

  1. Proof gates billing Invoice automation unlocks only after required photos attach when configured.
  2. Size limits Images auto-compressed so PDF + HTML email stay under provider send caps.
  3. Audit trail Timestamped proof on the job record — disputes reference the same packet managers received.
