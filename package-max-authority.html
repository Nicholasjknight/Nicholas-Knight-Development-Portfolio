Build process

  1. ResearchServices, markets, competitors, result pages, proof inventory, GBP status, and realistic page targets are reviewed.
  2. Individual page plansEvery page gets an intent, audience question, proof need, CTA, and internal-link role before build work starts.
  3. Conversion architectureService, area, proof, FAQ, estimate, and contact paths are designed as a system rather than isolated pages.
  4. Technical implementationCustom code, schema, metadata, performance, accessibility, measurement, forms, and GBP setup or optimization are implemented.
  5. Measurement and QAKey events are verified and the responsive, content, link, form, metadata, schema, and performance checks are completed before handoff.
  6. LaunchTwo revision rounds, production cutover, and a short minor-fix window. Monthly care remains optional and separate.

Max Authority FAQs

How many pages are included?

Up to 40 pages — PROJECT scope. Larger 60–100+ networks are Authority Network (from $6,500, SCOPED).

Does Max include monthly CRM?

No. Max is a project website. For tracker, sequences, and monthly ops, see Growth from $397/mo — including Systems-only if you already have a site.

Is GBP included?

A setup or optimization pass yes. Ongoing posts are Maintenance.

Can this become a Growth System later?

Yes. Many Max builds later add Systems-only Growth when ops become the bottleneck.

Why not Authority Network?

Authority Network is for 60–100+ page networks that need scoped architecture beyond Max. Max is the flat PROJECT cap at 40 pages for $4,500.

Will you create thin doorway pages?

No. Max is depth with architecture — service and area pages that support each other, not spam.
